## What changed since CSOL

The standard error is checked with a full-sample bootstrap of 1,000 resamples. AIPTW is repeated with 5-fold cross-fitting and three learners (logistic regression, LASSO, random forest). Overlap, weights and covariate balance are reported, including the effect among mothers who smoked. The ten-confounder model is compared with a pre-pregnancy-only set. Quitting and cigarettes per day are analysed.

## Major contribution

An absolute-risk estimate of the effect of smoking in pregnancy on preterm birth (3.26 percentage points, about 34 extra preterm births per 1,000 smoking pregnancies), tied to a causal diagram, with overlap checks, flexible cross-fitted models and an E-value.
